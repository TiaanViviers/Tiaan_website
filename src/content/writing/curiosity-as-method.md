Technical depth is usually described as narrowing. In practice, the people I admire most seem to narrow and widen in rhythm.

Statistics gives language for uncertainty. Computing turns that language into systems that survive contact with data. The rest of life—landscapes, long walks, nights under clear skies—reminds me that attention is a scarce resource and that pattern recognition is older than any library I will ever import.

Curiosity is not the opposite of rigour. It is how you decide which problems deserve rigour in the first place.
