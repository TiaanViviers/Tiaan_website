Bursary committees, internship reviewers, and engineering managers ask different questions, but they are sniffing for the same signal: can this person frame a problem, make trade-offs, and finish?

Code alone rarely answers that. Neither does polished prose with no artefacts. The useful middle ground is a case study posture—problem, constraints, approach, what failed, what remains unknown.

That is the standard I am trying to hold my own projects to.
